What Praxia PayCheck does

It estimates your NHS take-home pay, tax, National Insurance, pension and student loan for the 2026/27 tax year, across resident, consultant and SAS grades, plus a limited-company / locum route. Pick your grade, adjust the figures to match your payslip, and see the breakdown — all on your device.

Access codes

Praxia PayCheck is private. When you open it, enter the code you were given:

  • A full-access code unlocks the app permanently.
  • A 30-day code unlocks it for 30 days, after which you’ll be asked for a new code.

Enter a code once and it’s remembered on that device. No code? Ask whoever shared the app with you.

My figures don’t exactly match my payslip

Every field is editable. The auto-filled salary is the published national figure for your grade, but your actual pay depends on your exact hours, programmed activities, on-call, enhancements and local arrangements. Adjust the fields to match your payslip — the app is an estimate, not a payroll system.

Important: Praxia PayCheck is an estimator for general information only. It is not financial, tax or pension advice and gives no personal recommendation. Always check your payslip and speak to a qualified accountant or an FCA-authorised independent financial adviser before making any decision.
