Privacy Policy

Praxia PayCheck · last updated 27 September 2026

Praxia PayCheck does not collect any data.

The app runs entirely on your device. It has no account or sign-in, makes no analytics or tracking calls, and sends nothing to any server. There is no back-end, so there is simply nothing to collect, transmit, or leak.

What stays on your device

Anything you enter — pay figures, expenses, any payslip or bank-statement details you import, and your access code — is stored only in the app’s local storage on your own device. It is never uploaded. Deleting the app removes it.

Access codes

Access codes are checked on your device using a built-in verification. No code, and no information about how or when you use the app, is transmitted anywhere.

No third parties

No advertising, no third-party analytics or SDKs, no data sharing and no data selling. The app does not access your contacts, photos, location or health data.

Children

The app is intended for professional use by adults and is not directed at children. No data is collected from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
